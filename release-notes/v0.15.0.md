An agent working beside you can now read the listing on your screen and run a whole investigation from one command. A live watch also stays one clean table, whatever the size of your terminal.

- **Your listing, attached:** An MCP client can attach the listing your indexes resolve against, so you can ask "what's wrong with 3 in @kx:kx://listing?" without pasting the table.
- **Investigations as commands:** Four prompts run a triage, explain one resource, check a rollout or summarize image CVEs, as in `/kx:explain 3` or `/kx:triage prod` in Claude Code.
- **A watch that fits:** A watch with more rows than your terminal has lines draws the first ones that fit and counts the rest, as in `8 more rows don't fit — make the terminal taller to see them`.
- **Clean redraws:** A live table wider than your terminal, or one you resize while it runs, now redraws in place instead of leaving copies of itself in your scrollback.
