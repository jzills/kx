The `--limit` flag from v0.10.1 now reaches health sweeps and vulnerability scans, so the worst of a cluster is one command away.

- **The worst few resources:** A sweep can stop at its five worst: `kx diag -A --limit 5` numbers them 1 to 5, and its caption reads `5 of 21 shown`.
- **The most critical images:** A scan's summary can do the same with `kx scan -A --sort -crit,-high --limit 5`, though every image is still scanned.
- **The gate still reads everything:** With `--fail-on` beside `--limit`, a critical result past the cut still fails the run.
- **Latest runs for agents:** The `list_resources` tool on `kx mcp` takes `lastRun`, matching `kx get jobs --last-run`.
