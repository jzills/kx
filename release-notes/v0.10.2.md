The `--limit` that came to `kx get` and `kx top` in 0.10.1 now cuts a health sweep and a vulnerability scan too, so the worst few of a cluster are one command and one index away.

Running `kx diag -A --limit 5` keeps the first five rows the sweep shows, after its severity ranking and any `--sort`, so it is the five worst resources, numbered 1 to 5, and the rest of the sweep is neither saved nor reported. The caption counts the cut — `Mixed · all namespaces · 34 checked · 5 of 21 shown` — and so do `--json`, under a new `of` field, and the `--html` page. A `--fail-on` beside it still reads the whole sweep, so a critical resource past the cut fails the gate as before.

Running `kx scan -A --sort -crit,-high --limit 5` keeps the five most critical images of the summary. Every image is still scanned, and `--fail-on` still reads them all. The banner says `5 of 40 images`, and the `--json` document and the `--html` page record the cut the same way.

The `list_resources` tool on `kx mcp` takes `lastRun`, which lists the newest Job of each CronJob as `kx get jobs --last-run` does, with each row naming its CronJob. An agent asked whether last night's run failed can now find that run without matching Job names by eye.

Upgrading asks nothing of you. A `~/.kx/state.json` written by 0.10.1 resolves the same indexes, and a sweep or scan without `--limit` prints, saves and exits as it did.
