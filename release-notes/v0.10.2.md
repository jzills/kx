The `--limit` flag from 0.10.1 now cuts a health sweep and a vulnerability scan too, so the worst few of a cluster are one command and one index away.

- **The worst few resources:** Running `kx diag -A --limit 5` numbers the five worst resources 1 to 5, and the caption counts the cut: `Mixed · all namespaces · 34 checked · 5 of 21 shown`.
- **The most critical images:** Running `kx scan -A --sort -crit,-high --limit 5` keeps the five most critical images, though every image is still scanned.
- **The gate still reads everything:** A `--fail-on` beside `--limit` still reads the whole sweep or scan, so a critical result past the cut fails as before.
- **Latest runs for agents:** The `list_resources` tool on `kx mcp` takes `lastRun`, listing the newest Job of each CronJob as `kx get jobs --last-run` does.
