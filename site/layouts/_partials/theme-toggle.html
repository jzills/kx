Hextra's navbar has no extension point for a control of our own — it switches on a fixed set of menu item types — but "theme-toggle" is one of them, and the sidebar footer renders the same partial. Overriding the partial therefore puts this button in the navbar, in the sidebar and in the mobile menu at once, without copying navbar.html and inheriting the job of keeping that copy in step with upstream. One button, light or dark, rather than a menu of all ten palettes: a reader reaching for the navbar wants the page brighter or darker, and a ten-item menu in every page's chrome made the palettes look like site settings rather than what they are, a kx feature. The full set is on the landing page, under "In your colors", where it demonstrates kx theme. Light means the "light" palette. Deliberately not reusing Hextra's .hextra-theme-toggle class: its core/theme.js binds clicks on it and coerces anything outside ["light","dark"] to "system", which no kx palette name survives.
